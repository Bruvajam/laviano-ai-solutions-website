LAVIANO AI — LOGO FILES
=======================

The mark is a voice waveform: one tall bar (the caller) and three rising bars
(the reply). Same shape as the icon on your website.

WHICH FILE TO USE
-----------------
logo-horizontal-navy.png     The dark blue version. Self-contained navy plate,
   2002 x 576                rounded corners. Use this as your default —
                             works on any background, light or dark.
                             Good for: email signatures, invoices, letterheads,
                             social profile banners, slide decks.

logo-horizontal-on-dark.png  Same lockup, TRANSPARENT background, light artwork.
   1714 x 400                Use only on dark backgrounds.

logo-horizontal-on-light.png Same lockup, TRANSPARENT background, navy artwork.
   1714 x 400                Use only on white or light backgrounds.

logo-icon-512.png            Square app icon, navy tile. Use where a square is
logo-icon-1024.png           required: social profile pictures, favicons,
                             app stores, Google Business Profile.
                             1024 for anywhere that wants high resolution.

logo-mark.svg                Vector square icon. Scales to any size with no
logo-mark-on-light.svg       quality loss — use for print, large signage, or
logo-mark-on-dark.svg        anywhere a vector is accepted. The -on-light and
                             -on-dark versions are bars only, no tile.

PNG vs SVG: PNGs have the Playfair Display wordmark baked into the pixels, so
they look identical everywhere. SVGs are icon-only (no wordmark) because text
in an SVG needs the font installed to render correctly.

COLOURS
-------
Navy (tile, dark text)   #0f2a5c
Ink (wordmark on light)  #16202f
Blue (accent on light)   #0b57d6
Light blue (accent dark) #7fb2ff
Bar tints                #ffffff  #5f9dff  #7fb2ff  #a9ccff

USAGE
-----
- Keep clear space around the logo of at least the height of the tall bar.
- Do not stretch, recolour, rotate, or add effects.
- Do not place the transparent light version on a light background, or the
  dark version on a dark one — contrast disappears.
- Minimum width for the horizontal lockup is about 160px before the wordmark
  becomes hard to read. Below that, use the square icon instead.
